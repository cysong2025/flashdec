# FlashDec Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want FlashDec discussions and contributions to remain technically rigorous, welcoming, and safe for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss evidence, code, and design decisions without attacking people.
- Be precise about uncertainty, experimental scope, and negative results.
- Respect privacy and remove credentials, private paths, and unrelated logs from reports.
- Give constructive feedback and acknowledge other contributors' work.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope, safety, and evidence requirements.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal insults, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith argument.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Manipulating benchmark evidence, concealing relevant negative results, or knowingly making misleading performance claims.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing the FlashDec project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. Use a private contact method listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile when one is available. Otherwise, open a minimal issue requesting private follow-up without including names, screenshots, logs, or incident details.

The maintainer may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily limit participation, or permanently ban a participant depending on severity and recurrence. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, but confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when investigation or legal obligations require disclosure.
